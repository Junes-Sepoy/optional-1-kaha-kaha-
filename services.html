Services

Individual Therapy

Sometimes life can feel overwhelming in ways that are hard to explain. We’re here to support you through anxiety, depression, trauma, stress, and emotional overwhelm, while also offering assessments and diagnoses as part of your care.

Relationships & Family Therapy

Relationships can be beautiful, messy, comforting, and confusing, sometimes all at once.

Whether you’re navigating conflict with a partner, family tension, or communication struggles, we create a space where people can feel heard, understood, and more connected to one another.

Therapy for Teens & Young Adults

Growing up can feel like a lot sometimes. From school pressure and friendships to identity, family dynamics, and feeling unsure of yourself, we’re here to help you navigate it all.

Online Therapy

Whether you’re curled up at home or squeezing in a session during a busy week, support is always within reach. Therapy comes to you, wherever you are.

In-Person Therapy

Come in, settle down, and have a conversation that’s honest, grounding, and entirely about you. Our Bangalore and Delhi spaces are here for you when you need them.

Finding the Right Therapist

The right therapist can make all the difference. We’re here to help you find someone who feels like a good fit, someone you can slowly build trust and comfort with over time.

Workplace Wellness Programs

We collaborate with organizations to create wellness programs that prioritise emotional well-being, resilience, and healthier workplaces. We believe that mental health support should be part of every healthy workplace.

Mental Health Workshops

We host open conversations and interactive workshops for anyone looking to navigate the ups and downs of everyday life and learn a little more about themselves along the way.

Kaha Content & Community

We believe support can exist in small, everyday moments too. Through Instagram, LinkedIn, monthly mailers, and our newsletters, we share thoughtful mental health content you can come back to whenever you need it.

The Edge

This is a learning space for anyone growing into the work of being a therapist.

From undergraduate students to advanced practitioners, we offer training that supports both professional growth and deeper understanding.
